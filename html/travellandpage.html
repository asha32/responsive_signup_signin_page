1. Santorini & Mykonos, Greece

These iconic white-washed buildings with blue domes set against the Aegean Sea create one of the world’s most photographed landscapes.

The volcanic island of Santorini offers dramatic caldera views, with luxury hotels and restaurants perched along the cliff edges.

2. Provence, France (Lavender Season)

The lavender fields of Provence paint the landscape purple from mid-June to mid-August.

The region’s stone villages and local markets provide a perfect base for exploring the surrounding fields.

3. Amalfi Coast, Italy

The Amalfi Coast’s dramatic cliffs and colorful towns create one of Italy’s most stunning seaside landscapes.

The coastal road connects charming towns like Positano, Amalfi, and Ravello, each offering its own unique character and viewpoints.
